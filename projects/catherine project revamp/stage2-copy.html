Dataset

The dataset I am using is The Intersection of Domestic Violence, Race, Ethnicity, and Sex from NYC Open Data. It is stored in my dataset folder. Its associated data dictionary that provides a brief overview of the dataset and how the data are collected is also stored in my dataset folder.

Research Question

Based on the information provided in this dataset, what role, if any, does both the race and the sex of the suspect and the victim play in the occurrence of domestic violence incident reports in New York City?
