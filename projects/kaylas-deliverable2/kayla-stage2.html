Question 6 Which states had the highest proportion of Black incarceration in 2022, on average

#making a copy because I will be directly manipulating the df 
most_recent_year = most_recent_year.copy()

# creating the black proportion column

# accessing the incarcerated black column and incarcerated total and dividing to get the proportion
most_recent_year['black_proportion'] = (most_recent_year['incarcerated_black'] /
    most_recent_year['incarcerated_total']
    ) 
# creating a new df using the copy, grouping by state and filtered to black proportion and getting the mean of black proportion, then sorting it by highest to lowest
black_proportion_2022 = most_recent_year.groupby('state')['black_proportion'].mean().sort_values(ascending = False)
