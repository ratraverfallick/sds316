Children & Criminal Justice System Datasets

  • Survey of Youths in Custody, 1987 The survey provides detailed information on the characteristics of youths held primarily in secure settings within the juvenile justice system. The data contain information on criminal histories, family situations, drug and alcohol use, and peer group activities. For youths committed for violent acts, data are available on the victims of their crimes and on weapon use
  • Survey of Youth in Residential Placement (SYRP) 2003 [United States] According to the description, the Survey of Youth in Residential Placement (SYRP) is the only national survey that collects information directly from young people in the juvenile justice system. Developed by the Office of Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention (OJJDP), it uses anonymous interviews to ask youth aged 10 to 20 about their backgrounds, facility conditions, and personal experiences
  • National Survey of Youth in Custody, 2012 The National Survey of Youth in Custody collects confidential data on sexual assault in juvenile facilities to fulfill requirements of the Prison Rape Elimination Act of 2003. Using private audio computer assisted self interviews, the survey gathers detailed background, perception, and victimization information from thousands of youth across various facilities. These findings help the Bureau of Justice Statistics measure and report on the prevalence of sexual victimization in the juvenile justice system
  • Compendium of National Juvenile Justice Datasets The links aren’t working but I can guess that these data sets will be of use for my project once I find out how to get to them
  • JDAI DashboardState level data showing demographic information about Juveniles in Massachusetts prison. Also includes some data on youth held without bail.
